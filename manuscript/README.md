# Manuscript

This directory contains article drafts, supplementary methods, figure and table
plans, cover letters, reporting checklists, and reviewer responses.

## Current draft

`draft-01-preliminary/` contains the first computational manuscript draft based
on the manuscript outline, preliminary TE-localization results, and the
TE/ocean-acidification chapter of Kaisar Dauyey's 2024 PhD thesis.

The draft is deliberately separated from the section-level scaffold in
`../manuscript-outline.md`. Numerical results remain labelled preliminary until
the full result package, provenance, and robustness analyses are frozen.
